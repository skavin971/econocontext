Q741: smaller pages

All methods answered correctly. Ranking saved 25.6% of document output, but total model tokens increased 1.1% versus full because prompt/tool overhead offset the smaller payload.

Q333: a useful reduction

Full and ranked both answered copper, with extra wording. Ranked searched less and opened one document rather than two. Original paging hit the context limit. Savings are not attributable to ranking alone.

Q600: no ranked saving

Only original paging answered correctly. Ranked never opened a document; full opened one too late and exceeded context. Its zero document-output count is a failure signal, not an efficiency win.

*Q333 answers add qualifiers to “Copper”; manual comparison finds the requested colour equivalent, but strict exact-match is false. No independent model judge was run.

Methods: Full returns one complete allowed document view; Original pages preserve source order; Ranked pages mix chunks prioritized by agent-supplied topics and include uncalibrated relevance metadata. Same 100,195-document SQLite FTS5 corpus; document views capped at 8,192 source tokens. This is not official Lucene BCP reproduction.

Jev usage is separate from Qwen token counts. One independent stochastic search trajectory per method, with different queries and documents; no general causal claim or whole-system compute/dollar saving.
